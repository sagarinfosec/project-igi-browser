Controls & setup

Start with the default layout, then remap controls and tune aiming to suit your device.

Keyboard & mouse

  • Movement: use the default in-game movement keys, or choose the Modern (WASD) preset in Controls & settings.
  • Aim: move the mouse; click the game to capture the pointer if turning stops.
  • Fire: left mouse button by default; mouse buttons can be rebound.
  • Scope: right-click toggles the scope by default where the selected binding supports it.
  • Menu: Esc. Fullscreen: Alt + Enter or the fullscreen button.
  • Remap: click a displayed key in the controls panel and press the replacement key. Esc cancels; Backspace unbinds.

Phone & tablet

  • Rotate to landscape for the most usable play area.
  • Use the left virtual stick to move; slide the thumb onto the runner icon to lock sprint, and tap the stick to stop.
  • Swipe in the middle of the screen to aim. Hold Fire and drag to aim while shooting.
  • Use the surrounding buttons for scope/binoculars, crouch, jump and peek. The on-screen guide explains the current layout.
  • Open Settings to adjust touch sensitivity, gyro aiming and button placement/size/opacity.

Settings & performance

  • Controls and layout preferences are saved in the browser on this device.
  • Start with default sensitivity, then change one setting at a time.
  • Close other memory-heavy tabs and applications before loading a mission, especially on phones.
  • Allow motion access if you want to use gyro aiming and your browser asks for permission.
The exact options available depend on device type, browser support and the current game build. If a control is missing, open the in-game controls panel or see the FAQ.
